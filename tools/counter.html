What is a word counter?

A word counter analyzes text to show word counts, characters, and estimated reading time. It helps writers and SEO teams optimize content length.

How it works

ToolHub counts words and characters instantly as you type. Reading time is estimated using an average 200 words per minute.

Example

A 400-word article will show about 2 minutes of reading time.
