What is a UUID?

A UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit identifier used to uniquely label data across systems without coordination.

How it works

ToolHub generates v4 UUIDs in your browser, which are randomly generated and extremely unlikely to collide.

Example

A UUID looks like 3f2504e0-4f89-41d3-9a0c-0305e82c3301.
